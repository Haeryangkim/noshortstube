NoShortsTube

NoShortsTube is a YouTube client app for iPhone and Mac that hides all YouTube Shorts — browse trending, search, subscriptions, playlists, and your library with every Short filtered out.

What NoShortsTube does

NoShortsTube is a native SwiftUI YouTube client app for iPhone and Mac. Its single purpose is to let you browse and watch YouTube — trending, search, subscriptions, playlists, and your library — while automatically hiding every YouTube Short from every feed. Signing in with your Google account gives the app read-only access to your YouTube subscriptions, playlists, and library so it can display them inside the app; playback always uses the official YouTube player.

Format-based filtering

Shorts are detected by format, not just length: a 45-second landscape video stays visible, while a two-minute vertical Short is hidden. Playback uses the official YouTube embedded player.

Private by design

No servers, no tracking, no analytics. Your Google sign-in tokens stay in the system Keychain on your device, and your watch history is stored locally only. See our Privacy Policy.
