NoShortsTube

YouTube for iOS & macOS — with every Short filtered out.

What it is

NoShortsTube is a native SwiftUI YouTube client for iPhone and Mac. Sign in with your Google account to browse trending videos, search, and access your subscriptions, playlists, and library — just like YouTube, but with all Shorts hidden from every feed.

Format-based filtering

Shorts are detected by format, not just length: a 45-second landscape video stays visible, while a two-minute vertical Short is hidden. Playback uses the official YouTube embedded player.

Private by design

No servers, no tracking, no analytics. Your Google sign-in tokens stay in the system Keychain on your device, and your watch history is stored locally only. See our Privacy Policy.
