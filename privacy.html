Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

NoShortsTube ("the app") is a native YouTube client for iOS and macOS that hides Shorts. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored. The short version: everything stays on your device — we operate no servers and collect nothing.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with your Google account, the app requests access to your YouTube account data through the official YouTube Data API. This is used to show you:

  • Your subscriptions and the videos of channels you follow
  • Your playlists and library (liked videos, watch later)
  • Search results and trending videos

The app reads this data solely to display it to you inside the app.

Where your data lives

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in the operating system Keychain on your device and are sent only to Google's own API endpoints.
  • Watch history is recorded locally on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Settings and cached data (such as Shorts-detection results) are stored locally on your device.

What we do not do

  • We operate no backend servers; your data is never transmitted to us.
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.
  • We do not show ads or use tracking of any kind.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

NoShortsTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google APIs is used only to provide the app's user-facing features described above and is never used for advertising, resold, or transferred to third parties.

Revoking access

You can sign out at any time in the app's Settings, which deletes the stored tokens from your Keychain. You can also revoke the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data, including watch history.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information — from children or anyone else.
